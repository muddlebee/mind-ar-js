A private preview

Camera frames are processed locally by MindAR and MediaPipe. This application does not identify you, upload your frames, request a microphone, record, or add analytics.

Starting tracking downloads the pinned MediaPipe 0.10.9 WASM runtime from jsDelivr and Google's version 1 face-landmarker model. Those services receive ordinary download requests, not application camera images. An internet connection is required.

Credits & limitations

All ten effect graphics, thumbnails, and accessory geometries are original procedural artwork provided under the repository's MIT license. No bundled third-party models or SparkAR assets are used.

Built with MindAR, Three.js, and MediaPipe; their own licenses apply. The depth-only occluder uses MindAR's existing face geometry. License notices.

Visual play, not a physical fit measurement. One face at a time. Desktop Chrome and Android Chrome are physical-test targets; real-device results are pending. iPhone Safari is unverified. Ear placement and back-of-head occlusion are approximate.
