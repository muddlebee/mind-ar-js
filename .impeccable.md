## Design Context

### Users

Visitors exploring a public, no-sign-in face try-on demo, primarily on a phone. Developers also use the demo to understand and extend MindAR. The owner is learning the underlying AR, graphics, and browser-media concepts hands-on.

### Brand Personality

Playful, colorful, approachable. Encourage experimentation without making the interface feel like an ecommerce checkout or a complex developer dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

Camera-first filter playground. A large camera stage, clear category browsing, illustrated effect thumbnails, and a compact set of controls. Use warm neutral surfaces with a restrained bright accent palette. Mobile interaction is thumb-friendly; desktop layout can place the picker beside the camera. Avoid decorative effects that compete with the live face preview.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the live camera and selected effects visually dominant.
2. Make selection, replacement, removal, and Clear all immediately understandable.
3. Combine effects across categories; show loading, selection, and failures distinctly.
4. Make camera permission and privacy explicit. No sign-in, recording, or photo capture in v0.1.
5. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.

Current feature and delivery scope is defined in `docs/plans/face-try-on-playground.md`. No implementation or deployment is implied by this design-context file.
